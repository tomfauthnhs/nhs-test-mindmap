Card sort themes vs the current IA map: test ordering, invites and screening

Facts only. This page does not judge the design; it sets out what emerged in the card sort and where those items sit on the current map.

← Tree viewBy end pointConnected mapIdea feedsPlacement table
Source: Optimal Sort study "Testing in the App Card Sort" (shared results). 103 of 253 participants completed; 60 cards; 782 categories created; all UK; median time 14:41. Figures below are read from the Dendrograms, Cards and Similarity matrix tabs.
Scope: GET stage (find, access, book or order) and items related to test ordering, invites and screening. Prescriptions, vaccines, profile data, conditions and wellbeing apps are excluded. Tracking, measurements and results items are noted only where they appear next to GET items.
How to read the numbers: "pairwise similarity" = % of participants who placed two cards in the same category (similarity matrix). "% in Tests" etc. = share of the 103 completers who put that card in a category named that way, after Optimal Sort's category standardisation.

3. Cross-cutting patterns in the data

Blood tests group tightly

Blood and urine tests have the highest internal pairwise similarity of any group (mean 84%, up to 95%). They are 91-96% in Tests for 6 of 8 cards.

Kit, blood and imaging cards overlap in "Tests"

Kits vs blood: mean 77%. Imaging vs blood: mean 65%. Smear/mammogram vs blood: mean 67%. All have most placements in Tests.

Test vs Appointment split differs by card

Home kits and blood tests: 2-3% Appointments. Smear test: 12%. Breast screening and chest X-ray: 27% and 21%. 12-week scan: 31%. 6-8 week and postnatal checks: 47-48% Appointments (more than Tests).

Result and report cards split between Tests and Documents

Cervical result, bowel result, MRI report and mammography report each have 28-42% in Documents.

Booking an appointment sits apart from tests

"Check for available GP appointment": 99% Appointments, 0-4% pairwise similarity with blood tests, 11% with smear test.

A test and its result are not grouped strongly

Pairwise similarity of a test with its own result: cervical 45%, bowel 48%, breast 40%, HIV 63%.

Home monitoring cards split

Home blood pressure monitor: 46% Tests / 33% Health conditions. CGM: 52% Tests / 31% Health conditions.

Closest to the map vs furthest

Items with matching map entries (screening, Health Check, appointments, documents) vs items with no entry (blood and urine tests as NHS.uk end points, imaging, HPV kit, home monitoring, 12-week scan, 6-8 week and postnatal checks).

4. Caveats

• Percentages are read from Optimal Sort's standardised categories and the dendrogram; they are approximate and rounded.
• "Tests" contains both GET items and RESULT items, so the % in Tests alone does not separate them.
• The card sort did not test: opticians, AAA, diabetic eye screening, Jewish BRCA, pharmacy blood pressure check, 20-week scan, Messages.
• Card wording (for example "home test") may have influenced grouping.
• The card sort tested 60 items; the map has 20 NHS.uk end points plus NHS App areas. Only overlapping items are compared here.
• The matrix gives pairwise similarity; it does not show category names chosen by participants for each cluster.
