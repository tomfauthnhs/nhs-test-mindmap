Key takeaways

  1. Blood tests and home kits were placed in "Tests" by almost everyone. 8 blood and urine cards: 75-100% in Tests. 6 home test kits: 85-100% in Tests (HPV self-sampling kit lowest). Fewer than 5 of 103 put either group in Appointments.
  2. Blood and urine tests have no NHS.uk entry point on the current map. They appear only in the Book a GP appointment note, GP/hospital ordered results and the in-build "Arrange a ___ test" journey. Imaging, the HPV self-sampling kit, home blood pressure monitoring, CGM, the 12-week scan and 6-8 week and postnatal checks are also not on the map.
  3. Screening, Health Check and most pregnancy/newborn tests are on the map. Cervical and breast screening (clinic), bowel, AAA, diabetic eye, Health Check, pregnancy, 20-week scan and newborn tests (invited).
  4. Participants were split on whether some items are a test or an appointment. 6-8 week check, postnatal check and health visitor check: about half placed them in Appointments, about a quarter in Tests. Breast screening 31% in Appointments; 12-week scan 36%.
  5. Results and reports were split between Tests and Documents. Cervical, bowel and HIV results, and MRI and mammography reports: 21-48% placed them in Documents. MRI report: Documents 49 vs Tests 48.
  6. Letters and appointment notes were split between Documents and Appointments. GP letter 55 Documents / 53 Appointments; hospital letter 63 / 49; appointment notes 55 / 49.
